Who We Are

Vision & Mission

Rooted in compassion, guided by purpose. Swan Foundation stands for an equitable world where every individual can thrive.

🔭

Our Vision

A just and inclusive society where every person — regardless of gender, caste, or economic background — has access to quality education, clean environment, healthcare, and livelihood opportunities. We envision communities that are self-reliant, informed, and empowered to build their own futures.

🎯

Our Mission

To catalyse sustainable change at the grassroots by implementing community-led programmes in education, women empowerment, environment conservation, and healthcare — fostering dignity, resilience, and self-sufficiency in every life we touch. We work transparently, collaboratively, and with deep respect for local wisdom.

What We Do

Our Projects

From classrooms to clinics, from forests to factories — here's where Swan Foundation is making change happen.

📖
Education

Vidya Prakash – Community Library

Setting up mobile libraries across 6 slum pockets in Mumbai, bringing books, digital tablets and tutoring to 420+ children who lack access to quality education.

Since Jan 2023
Beneficiaries 420 kids

78% funded · Goal ₹8,00,000

🌿
Environment

HaritaShakti – Urban Forest Revival

Partnering with municipal bodies to plant 10,000 native species trees across Mumbai's eastern suburbs — restoring green lungs and building community ownership of urban ecology.

Since Mar 2023
Trees Planted 5,600+

56% complete · 4,400 trees remaining

👩‍⚕️
Women Empowerment

Udaan – Skill & Self-Reliance

Vocational training in tailoring, computer literacy, and micro-enterprise management for 200 women from marginalised households — building financial independence from the ground up.

Since Jun 2023
Women Trained 200

90% funded — almost there!
